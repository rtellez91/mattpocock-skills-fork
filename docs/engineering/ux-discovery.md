## What it does

Choose a UX method and useful fidelity for a concrete product uncertainty, or assess design readiness from existing evidence. It supports the current interview rather than creating another mandatory phase.

## When to reach for it

The agent can load this during product interviews, Wayfinder, prototype work, specification, ticketing, or readiness checks. You may also invoke `/ux-discovery` directly.

## Prerequisites

Supply the available product evidence and current design resources. Visual exploration requires the separate Product Design plugin; assessment and nonvisual discovery can run without it.

## Common questions

**Do I have to know which UX method to choose?**

No. The agent explains its choice, the question it addresses, and the evidence needed to make a decision. Product tradeoffs and visual selection remain yours.

## It's working if

An internal change gets a proportionate applicability check; a reused pattern does not trigger unnecessary mockups; a missing design decision gates only the affected work.
